# 🦉🐊💻 Universal Wisdom School - Grade 2 SA-1 Exam Prep App

> **Duolingo-style Mobile Web App** for **Grade 2 Summative Assessment 1 (SA-1)**.
> Features a dynamic **3-Subject Switcher** toggling between:
> 1. 🇬🇧 **English Language** — 30 Marks (45 mins) matching textbook **Hodder Cambridge Primary English Stage 2** (Units 1 & 2 + Language Topics).
> 2. 💻 **Computing Theory (कम्प्यूटिंग)** — 20 Marks matching **Cambridge Primary Computing** (Units 1, 2, and 3).
> 3. 🇮🇳 **Hindi (हिंदी)** — 30 Marks (20M Theory + 5M Listening + 5M Speaking) matching textbook **"बाल पतवार २"**.

### 2. 🇬🇧 English Language Courseware (Hodder Stage 2 • 30 Marks)
Directly aligned with the school SA-1 syllabus and Hodder Cambridge Primary English Stage 2 textbook chapters:

#### 🗺️ 5 Adventure Path Units:
1. **Unit 1: Roald Dahl & Characters** 🎩
   - Character study: Mr. Willy Wonka (plum-coloured velvet tailcoat, bottle green trousers, gold-topped cane, goatee beard, bright sparkling eyes) & Mr. Bucket (toothpaste factory worker).
   - 4 Character aspects: What they look like, what they do (actions), what they say/think, how they feel.
   - Adjective placement rules: placed *before* nouns (*"a narrow room"*) or *after* nouns with linking verbs (*"The room was narrow"*).
2. **Unit 2: The Enormous Crocodile & Settings** 🐊
   - The Enormous Crocodile vs The Notsobig One (*"I am going to eat a child for lunch"* vs *"Fish are better"*).
   - Story settings: Place, time, and weather.
   - Contrasting settings: Sunny park (*bright, warm, cheerful*) vs Stormy forest (*gloomy, thundery, dark, cold*).
   - Sensory adjectives for setting mood.
3. **Unit 3: Story Mountain & Plot Stages** ⛰️
   - 6 Stages of a Story Mountain:
     1. Introduction (meet characters & setting)
     2. Problem (something goes wrong / secret plans)
     3. Development (events build up)
     4. Climax / Most Exciting Moment (turning point)
     5. Falling Action (consequences unfold)
     6. Ending (solution / resolved)
   - Roald Dahl's inspiration for *Charlie and the Chocolate Factory* (Cadbury sending test chocolate bars to boarding school boys).
4. **Unit 4: Speech Marks & Dialogue** 💬
   - Inverted commas (`"..."`) enclosing only the exact words spoken out loud.
   - Punctuating direct speech with internal commas, exclamation marks, or question marks before closing quotation marks.
   - Better words for said: *whispered, shouted, snorted, cried, gasped, muttered, asked*.
5. **Unit 5: Party Instructions, Direction Words & Compounds** 🎈
   - Writing lists down the page with bullet points or numbers.
   - Pop-up card instructional text structure: Title, 'You will need' materials list, numbered chronological steps, clear diagrams.
   - Invitations: Who, Where, When, What, and **RSVP** (*Répondez s'il vous plaît* / Please reply).
   - Giving clear directions: short sentences, sequencing words (*First, Next, Then, Finally*), and command verbs (*Turn left, Walk past*).
   - Suffix spelling rules (`-ed` and `-ing`):
     - Drop silent 'e' before adding suffix: *smile ➔ smiled, bake ➔ baking, dance ➔ dancing*.
     - Double the final consonant after a short vowel: *hop ➔ hopped, sit ➔ sitting, clap ➔ clapped*.
     - Exception: Never double words ending in *w*, *x*, or *y* (*play ➔ playing, box ➔ boxing*).
   - Compound words: Joining two root words (*playground, bedroom, toothbrush, football, scarecrow, anywhere*).
   - Proofreading rules: Checking grammar (makes sense), spelling, and punctuation (capital letters & full stops).

#### 🎮 6 English Practice Modes:
1. 📖 **Story Mountain & Characters** — Willy Wonka traits, crocodile plot sequencing & story mountain steps.
2. 📝 **Nouns, Verbs & Adjectives** — Parts of speech, adjective placement before/after nouns.
3. ✨ **Suffix Magic (-ed & -ing)** — Consonant doubling, dropping silent 'e', and exception rules.
4. 💬 **Speech Marks & Dialogue** — Inverted commas, direct speech punctuation & expressive dialogue verbs.
5. 📢 **Bossy Verbs & Instructions** — Command verbs for recipes, directions, pop-up cards & RSVP etiquette.
6. 🧩 **Compound Words** — Combining root words into everyday compound words.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Double-click `index.html` in your file explorer, or run a local static web server:
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
# Open in browser: http://localhost:8080
```

## 🌐 1-Click Deployment to GitHub Pages

1. Push to your GitHub repository:
   ```bash
   git add .
   git commit -m "Add English courseware (30 Marks), 3-way subject switcher, and custom SVGs"
   git push origin main
   ```
2. In your GitHub repository:
   - Go to **Settings** > **Pages**.
   - Under **Build and deployment** > **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
   - Select branch **`main`** and folder **`/ (root)`**, then click **Save**.
3. Within 1 minute, your Grade 2 Exam Prep App will be live on your phone via GitHub Pages!
